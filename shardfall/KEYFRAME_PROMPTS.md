# SHARDFALL: prompts to redraw the 12 keyframes

Output 1920×1080 (16:9) or larger, PNG. Keep each shot's composition as described: the animation's cameras, effects and timing are aimed at those positions.

Make **shot 03 (Ren)** and **shot 04 (Hana)** first, then feed them as character references (image prompt / reference / IP-Adapter) into every other shot so faces, hair and costumes stay consistent.

## Put before every prompt

> Gacha-game splash-art illustration, highly detailed anime character art, fine individual hair strands with glossy highlights, soft painted cel shading with gradient shadows, intricate costume ornaments (gold filigree, crystal gems, fur trim, flowing ribbons), rim lighting, luminous eyes with layered irises, cinematic painted background with volumetric light and drifting particles. Fine thin line art with varied line weight: thicker outer contour, very thin inner lines for hair strands and cloth folds; coloured lines instead of black (dark teal on green fabric, warm brown on skin and gold, deep blue-grey on dark hair), lines fading lighter where light hits; crisp clean edges. 16:9.

## Negative

> thick uniform black outlines, flat vector shapes, chibi, sketchy lines, 3D render, photorealistic, distorted face, extra limbs, extra fingers, text, watermark, logo, low quality

## Characters

- **Ren**: teenage boy, spiky dark-teal hair with fine strands and a lighter teal streak, sharp amber eyes, calm and focused. Long dark-green coat with gold-trimmed lapels, crystal clasps and a fur collar, white shirt, red tie. Weapon: a short blade of translucent cyan light-glass with a gold guard.
- **Hana**: teenage girl, long navy hair in a high side ponytail tied with a yellow silk ribbon, bright violet eyes, big confident grin. Matching long green coat with white fur cuffs and gold crescent ornaments. Weapon: wooden staff with a faceted cyan crystal tip and gold rings.
- **The Glass Beast**: huge four-legged creature of overlapping translucent cyan crystal plates, light refracting inside, glowing white eyes, shards flaking off its edges.
