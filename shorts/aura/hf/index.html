It had rained
in Sol for
nine years.
One man never
got wet.
They called him
Aura Monster.
Midnight.
No umbrella.
Bone dry.
The shopkeepers said
luck.
The police said
a trick.
The kids said
magic.
So one night,
I followed him
into the alleys.
Then I saw
it.
He wasn't stopping
the rain.
He was dodging
it.
Every drop.
Too fast for
the storm to
touch him.
He turned.
"You'll catch a
cold,
kid."
"Teach me," I
said.
So he did.
Every night,
in the rain.
I held my
umbrella.
I couldn't dodge
yet.
A hundred nights.
A thousand drops.
Then one night,
I closed it.
Now there are
two of us.
And we never
get wet.
