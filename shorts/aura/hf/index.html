It had rained
in Sol for
nine years.
One man never
got wet.
They called him
Aura monster.
Midnight,
no coat,
no hat,
bone dry.
The shopkeepers said
luck.
The police said
a trick.
The kids said
magic.
So one night
I followed him.
The rain stopped
around me.
Then I saw
why.
He wasn't dodging
the storm.
He was holding
it up,
one walk at
a time,
so the city
could sleep.
He turned,
tired,
and smiled.
Your turn.
Now I walk
these streets at
midnight.
And I never
get wet.
