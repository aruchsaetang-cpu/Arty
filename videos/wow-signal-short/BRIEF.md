## Intent

Hollow Truth File 004: the Wow! signal (August 15, 1977, Big Ear radio telescope,
Ohio). One world: a pulsing point in the star field. The camera dives into it and
comes out on the green-bar printout, where the 6EQUJ5 column is circled in red pen
and "Wow!" is written beside it. Then a bar chart of the signal's real strength, letter
by letter, then the sky near Sagittarius with a search beam sweeping and nothing
found. Ends on "WHO SENT IT?"

## Script (voiceover)

1. In 1977, a radio telescope caught a signal from deep space.
2. It was so strong, the astronomer circled it, and wrote one word.
3. Wow.
4. It lasted seventy-two seconds.
5. We've searched that patch of sky ever since.
6. It never came back.

- assets/audio/voiceover.m4a: Kokoro TTS (am_michael, 1.05x), pitched down 7% with

## Notes

- Everything is drawn. The printout digits around the 6EQUJ5 column are made up for
  texture; the 6EQUJ5 sequence and its values (6, 14, 26, 30, 19, 5) are real.
- The teapot is drawn near the source to say "the direction of Sagittarius". It isn't
  plotted to sky coordinates.
