**Title:** 3 Lighthouse Keepers Vanished Without a Trace 🌊 #shorts

December 1900. On the remote Flannan Isles off Scotland, three lighthouse keepers vanished.

The light had gone dark. The door and gate were shut. Two sets of oilskins were gone, but the third was still on its hook. So one man went out into the storm without his coat.

None of them were ever found.
