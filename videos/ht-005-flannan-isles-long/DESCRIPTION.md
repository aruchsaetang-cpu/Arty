**Title:** 3 Lighthouse Keepers Vanished… The Flannan Isles Mystery

December 1900. On the remote Flannan Isles, twenty miles off the west coast of Scotland, three lighthouse keepers vanished without a trace.

The light had gone dark. The door and gate were closed, the lamp was ready for the night, and one man's oilskins were still on the hook. At the west landing, railings were twisted and a heavy box was torn loose more than 100 feet above the sea.
