## Script (voiceover)

1. In December 1900, three men vanished from a lighthouse on one of the loneliest islands in Britain. No bodies. No struggle. No explanation.
2. The Flannan Isles sit about twenty miles off the west coast of Scotland. The lighthouse had only been lit a year earlier.
3. The island is tiny and treeless, with sheer cliffs on every side. Getting on and off meant climbing steps cut into the rock.
4. [THE LIGHT GOES OUT] On the fifteenth of December, a passing ship noticed something wrong. The light was out.
5. Three keepers were stationed there. James Ducat, Thomas Marshall, and Donald MacArthur.
6. A fourth keeper rotated in and out by boat. On the day the relief came, it was his turn to return. His name was Joseph Moore.
7. Storms kept the relief boat away until the twenty sixth of December. No flag was flying. No one came down to meet it.
8. [WHAT THEY FOUND] The relief keeper climbed up to the station. The door and the gate were closed. The lamp had been cleaned and filled, ready for the night.
9. Moore searched every room, and then the whole island, calling their names. There was no one.
10. Two sets of oilskins were gone. The third was still on its hook. One man had gone out without his coat.
11. The last entries on the keepers' slate were from the fifteenth. After that, nothing.
12. At the west landing, railings were twisted, and a heavy box of ropes had been torn from a crevice more than a hundred feet above the sea.
13. [THE THEORIES] The official theory: the men went down to secure equipment in a storm, and a huge wave swept them off the rocks.
14. But why would one man run out without his coat? Maybe he saw the others in trouble, and ran to help.
15. Others have suggested a fight, or madness from the isolation. But there was no sign of either.
16. Over the years, the story grew. A famous poem in 1912 added ghostly details that were never in the real reports.
17. Their bodies were never found. Today the light runs automatically. No one lives on the island at all.
