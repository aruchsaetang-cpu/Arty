**Title:** A Hum Only 2% of People Can Hear 👂 #shorts

In Taos, New Mexico, some people hear a low hum that never stops. Only about 2% of the people there can hear it.

In the 1990s, scientists from national labs investigated. They couldn't find the source. People around the world have reported the same hum.
