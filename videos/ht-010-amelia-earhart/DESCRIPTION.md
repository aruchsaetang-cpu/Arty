**Title:** Amelia Earhart's Last Radio Call 📻 #shorts

July 1937. Amelia Earhart was trying to fly around the world, with about 7,000 miles left to go.

Her last radio call said: "We are running north and south." Then, silence, over the middle of the Pacific.

Neither she, her navigator Fred Noonan, nor her plane has ever been found.
