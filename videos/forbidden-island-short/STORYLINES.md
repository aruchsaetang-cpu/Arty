# Storylines

1. **The Chart (built).** A nautical chart is the one world, and the camera flies
   through it. Each fact is drawn onto the chart: the no-go ring, a 60,000-year
   count-up, the 2004 helicopter and the arrow, the 2018 canoe wake that stops on
   the beach, and a "?" over the island.
2. **Case File.** A redacted government dossier: stamps, file photos, typewriter
   reveals of each date.
3. **Countdown.** Type alone carries it: giant numbers (3 miles, 60,000, 2004,
   2018, ?) cut on the beat over color fields.
