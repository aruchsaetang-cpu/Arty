**Title:** Two Men, Lead Masks, and a Strange Note 🎭 #shorts

1966. Two men were found dead on a hill in Brazil, wearing suits, raincoats, and masks made of lead.

Next to them, a notebook said: "4:30 PM, be at the agreed place. 6:30 PM, swallow capsules. After effect, protect metals. Wait for mask signal."

No cause of death was ever found.

What were they waiting for? 👇
