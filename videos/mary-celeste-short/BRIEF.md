## Intent

Hollow Truth File 003: the Mary Celeste (found adrift December 4, 1872, east of
the Azores). A brigantine drifts in moonlit fog with nobody at the helm. The camera
moves to the empty deck, into the cabin (the hull opens in cutaway, table still laid),
to the empty davits, out to an empty sea, and back to the ship drifting on.
Ends on "WHY DID THEY LEAVE?"

## Script (voiceover)

1. In 1872, a ship was found drifting in the Atlantic.
2. Nobody on board. Not one of the ten people.
3. Six months of food and water. Their belongings untouched.
4. The only lifeboat was gone.
5. They were never seen again.
6. So why would ten people abandon a perfectly good ship?
