**Title:** A 3,700-Year-Old Disc No One Can Read 🌀 #shorts

In 1908, archaeologists in Crete found a clay disc covered in symbols. It's around 3,700 years old.

The symbols were stamped into the clay, like an early printing press. There are 242 of them, spiraling to the center.

No one has ever deciphered it.
