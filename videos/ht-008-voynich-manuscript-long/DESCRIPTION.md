**Title:** The 600-Year-Old Book No One Can Read: The Voynich Manuscript

There's a book at Yale that no one on Earth can read. The Voynich manuscript is about 240 pages of vellum, carbon dated to the early 1400s, filled with plants no botanist can identify, star charts, and women bathing in strange green pools.

It's written in a script found nowhere else. Top codebreakers, including World War Two experts, have tried and failed.
