**Title:** 5 Children Vanished on Christmas Eve 🏚️ #shorts

Christmas Eve, 1945. A fire destroyed the Sodder family home in West Virginia. Five of their children were never seen again, and no bodies were found in the ashes.

Their parents believed the children were still alive, and put up a billboard by the road asking for help. It stood there for decades.
