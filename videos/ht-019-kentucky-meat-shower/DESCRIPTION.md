**Title:** The Day It Rained Meat in Kentucky 🥩 #shorts

March 3, 1876. It rained meat in Kentucky. Chunks of red flesh fell from a clear sky over a farm, some up to four inches across.

Two men tasted it. They said it was mutton, or venison.

The best guess? Vultures, throwing up as they flew over.
