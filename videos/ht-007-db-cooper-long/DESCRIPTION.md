**Title:** He Hijacked a Plane, Jumped With $200,000… and Vanished. The D.B. Cooper Mystery

November 24, 1971. A quiet man in a dark suit hijacked Northwest Orient Flight 305 with a note saying he had a bomb. He collected $200,000 and four parachutes, then jumped from the rear stairs into a freezing, rainy night over the forests of Washington.

In 1980, a boy found $5,800 of the ransom by the Columbia River. The man himself was never found.
