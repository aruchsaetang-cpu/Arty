**Title:** He Hijacked a Plane, Then Jumped… and Vanished 🪂 #shorts

November 1971. A man in a suit hijacked a plane with a briefcase he said held a bomb. He got $200,000 and four parachutes.

Then he jumped out the back of the plane, into the night.

In 1980, a boy found $5,800 of the cash buried by the Columbia River. The man himself was never found.
