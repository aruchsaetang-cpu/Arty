**Title:** 115 Settlers Vanished. One Word Was Left Behind. The Lost Colony of Roanoke

In 1587, about 115 English settlers landed on Roanoke Island. Their governor, John White, sailed home for supplies, and war with Spain kept him away for three years.

When he returned in August 1590, on his granddaughter's third birthday, everyone was gone. No bodies, no sign of a battle, and no distress cross. Just one word carved into a post: CROATOAN.
