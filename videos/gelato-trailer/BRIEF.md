## Intent

A 30-second gelato ad starring the person in the user's photo, cut like a
Hollywood blockbuster trailer: "In a world…" cards, a silhouette hero reveal,
a montage, a title slam and a billing-block end card.

## Assets

  (passers-by and a stray leaf cleaned out).
- assets/audio/trailer-score.m4a — trailer score synthesised for this cut
  (drone, braams, hits, riser, slam), -14 LUFS.
