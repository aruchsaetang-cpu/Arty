**Title:** 5 Navy Planes Vanished… Then the Rescue Plane Did Too. Flight 19

December 5, 1945. Five Navy Avenger bombers took off from Fort Lauderdale on a training flight. Their leader radioed that both of his compasses had failed. Night fell, a storm moved in, and they were never seen again.

A rescue flying boat with 13 men went looking for them, and vanished too. Six aircraft, 27 men, in one evening. No wreckage was ever found.
