## Sources / verification
- Date/place: 12 June 2014, Pinewood Studios; Ford 71; broke left leg (tibia+fibula), ankle dislocated; airlifted to John Radcliffe Hospital Oxford (BBC via Slashfilm/Screencrush; HSMSearch; Maxim).
- Door force "similar to the weight of a small car", no automatic emergency cut-off: HSE statement reported by Huffington Post UK, Looper, SHP Online. Foodles Production (UK) Ltd fined GBP 1.6m, Aylesbury Crown Court, Oct 2016.
- Ford pressed the door button during a dress rehearsal, not expecting it to close with cameras off: Huffington Post UK (HSE account). Script says only "he pressed the close button".
- Production paused: sources say two weeks to nearly three weeks, so the script says "หลายสัปดาห์".
- Abrams: "greatest gift to the movie", pause let them rework scenes: TheWrap, Slashfilm.
- HOOK WORDING: no source says Ford was in medical danger of death; Vice/Comicbook headlines say "nearly killed". The hook uses "โดนประตูยานทับ" (verified). Alternative if wanted: "Han Solo เกือบตายจริงๆ ในกองถ่าย" (headline-level claim).
- Not verified: which exact scene was being rehearsed; whether an object other than the door hit him (some outlets say a section of the set). Fine: the script says "ประตูยาน", the HSE wording.
