**Title:** A 2,000-Year-Old Computer Found in a Shipwreck ⚙️ #shorts

In 1901, divers pulled a lump of corroded bronze from an ancient Greek shipwreck. Inside were dozens of tiny, precise gears.

It tracked the sun and moon and predicted eclipses, and it's over 2,000 years old. Nothing this complex appears again for more than a thousand years.
