## Intent

Hollow Truth File 002: the Dyatlov Pass incident (February 1959, northern Urals).
A torch beam searches a snowy slope at night. It finds the tent cut from the inside,
follows the footprints down to the cedar and the forest edge, then the camera pulls
back to reveal the mountain, Kholat Syakhl, "Dead Mountain". Ends on "WHAT HAPPENED?"

## Script (voiceover)

1. In 1959, nine hikers cut their tent open. From the inside.
2. Then they ran into the snow, barely dressed, at minus thirty.
3. Some had crushed chests, but almost no wounds on the skin.
4. One was missing her tongue.
5. Some of their clothes were radioactive.
6. And the mountain's name? Dead Mountain.
