**Title:** The Town That Couldn't Stop Dancing 💃 #shorts

July 1518. A woman in Strasbourg started dancing in the street, and she didn't stop for days. Within weeks, dozens of people had joined her.

Officials decided more dancing would cure it, so they built a stage and hired musicians.

No one knows what made them dance.
