# Channel style (applies to every new video)

Reference: **Sunnah Edit** Shorts (12M–149M views each). Our channel is now **heartbreaking /
emotional true-moment Shorts**.

> **Template:** `videos/bus-driver-baby/` is the approved reference build (freeze+zoom hook, 2-word instant hook, shutter+ding). Start every new
> video by copying its `index.html` and swapping footage, captions and circle positions; keep its
> timing, sound placement, impact, heart and loop exactly.

## Hard rules

- **Length: 14–30s** (fit the story; never longer than 30s). The five beats scale to the length; the 0.93s hit stays fixed. Vertical 1080×1920. Only the real moment itself: no talking heads, no photo collages, no filler.
- **No voice.** No narration, no TTS. Emotion comes from picture, text and music only.
- **One story per video**, built as a three-act arc: setup (who is it, what's wrong) →
  the turn (the painful or kind moment) → payoff (the tears, the reunion, the reveal).
- **Original topics.** Pick angles that the big edit channels have *not* done to death
  (see the topic bank below). Skip "man feeds hungry dog" and "boy gets surprise bike";
  those are saturated.

**Cut rhythm: a new shot every ~3 seconds.** No single shot runs longer than 3s (the 0.93s freeze-zoom counts as its own beat). Split long footage into several 3s cuts (different moments, angles or crops).

## Story structure (every video, 30s)

| Time | Beat | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **0–2s** | Strongest emotional image + hook | Plain footage of the strongest moment until the 0.93s hit (shutter + ding, freeze + zoom, 2-word hook) |
| **2–7s** | Introduce the person and situation | Who they are, what they are going through (lead with the loss/conflict) |
| **7–15s** | Reveal an unexpected detail | The twist nobody expects (the "But…") |
| **15–24s** | Explain the emotional turning point | The moment it changes: the kindness, the reunion, the tears |
| **24–30s** | Deliver the most meaningful fact or ending | Impact + heart on the strongest frame, the verdict line, loop back to the start |

**Principles (Sunnah Edit style):** real moments with a clear story (someone struggles → someone responds → the outcome); emotion before explanation; **cuts follow the music's emotional build**, not constant flashy transitions; simple text with one memorable theme (kindness, sacrifice, friendship, perseverance). Favor stories of **someone helping a stranger when nobody else does**.

**Core story formula (copied from Sunnah Edit): SOMEONE HELPS SOMETHING.** Every video is one helper and one helpless subject (a person or an animal):

1. **The helpless one:** show them in trouble; circle + label them (`HELPLESS DOG`, `STUCK DOG`, `BABY`).
2. **The helper appears:** arrow + label on the helper (`GOOD MAN`, `REAL SUPERMAN`, `DRIVER`).
3. **The help:** the act of kindness itself, the longest beat.
4. **The outcome:** the subject is safe → impact + heart → one meaningful closing line (never "SALUTE": it looks cheap). Use the strongest fact or a short truth: `*17 STRANGERS. ONE HUMANITY.*`, `*HIS HEART STILL BEATS*`, `*ONE STOP. ONE LIFE.*`.

These four steps sit inside the five-beat timing and the 3s cut rule.

**Idea source: copy and develop Sunnah Edit's ideas.** Pick topics from Sunnah Edit's top Shorts (e.g. *DOG IN DANGER*, *REAL SUPERMAN*, *THE LAST GOODBYE*, *SALUTE TO THIS BOND*), then develop them: find the **original source footage** of that event (news, the uploader's own video), or a different real event with the same theme, and tell it with our own five-beat structure, captions and edit. Never download or re-upload Sunnah Edit's edited video itself.

**Reused-content rule:** never just compile others' clips with music. Add original storytelling (structure, captions, edit), credit the source, and prefer footage you own, have permission for, or can lawfully reuse; YouTube can limit monetization for reused content.

All beats are on-screen captions (no voice), short lines in `*ASTERISKS*`.

Keep every line ≤ 6 words so it can be read at a glance.

### Hook rules (learned from the weak Gallop hook)

1. **Open on the strongest frame of the story** (tears, the rescue, the reunion), never a calm setup shot. Use it for the plain 0–0.93s too.
2. **Exactly 2 words, shown instantly on the hit (no typing), emotional label style** like the reference: `*SAD MOMENT*`, `*DOG IN DANGER*`, `*REAL SUPERMAN*`, or a curiosity gap (`*WHO IS HE?*`). Never an information sentence.
3. **Fully readable on the 0.93s hit frame**: the 2-word hook appears all at once with the flash.
3b. **Freeze + zoom on the hit**: at 0.93s the video freezes (still frame `assets/img/freeze.jpg`) and zooms ~1.9× into the subject for ~1.5s, with the circle on the subject, then the video continues. Built in `videos/bus-driver-baby/index.html` (`#frz`).
4. **The circle asks the question**: circle the person/animal the hook is about, with a 1–2 word label (`HERO`, `THE BABY`).
0. **Frame 1 = the real event or a frame that makes people curious, immediately.** The very first frame of every clip is the actual incident (the danger, the rescue, the tears) or a puzzling image that makes viewers ask "what is happening?". Never a title card, logo, interview, talking head, establishing shot or black frame.
5. **Open on emotion, not backstory.** Start on the shocking, sad or curious moment itself, never on history or setup. Backstory comes later as Context.
6. **Open loop.** The hook hints at what viewers want to know but does NOT answer it. Hold the answer until the Reveal (e.g. `*WHY IS THIS BABY ALONE?*`, `*WHAT SHE DID NEXT…*`).

**Lead with a relatable emotional conflict, loss or regret.** The hook and first Context lines frame something everyone has felt (a goodbye they did not get, a parent they did not call, a pet that waited, a kindness they wish they had shown), before the kindness or reunion pays it off.

**Topic focus:** real situations of human kindness: strangers or ordinary people helping someone, caught on real footage (bodycam, dashcam, CCTV, phone, news).

## Editing style (copied from the reference)

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Hook text | Appears at 0.0–0.5s, centred mid-frame, wrapped in asterisks: `*SAD MOMENT*`. Heavy condensed sans (Anton / Montserrat Black), ALL CAPS, black stroke + soft glow. |
| Colours | Neon green `#39FF14` for kindness/good, yellow `#FFE600` for danger/attention, white for neutral lines, red only for one word of loss. Two-tone lines are common: line 1 white, line 2 green. |
| Text entrance | Keyboard/typewriter: every caption types in letter by letter (~24 letters/s). No explosive scale-in or slam on captions. |
| Callouts | Yellow circle drawn around the subject + curved arrow pointing at it, with a short label: `GOOD MAN`, `HELPLESS DOG`, `REAL BIKE`, `POOR MAN`. Pops in with a scale bounce. |
| Year / place stamp | Big white `2016` or `INDIA, 2019` over the footage for "true story" weight. |
| Watermark | Channel name, semi-transparent white, centre, lower third, on for the whole video. |
| Grade | Punchy: high contrast, +saturation, slight sharpen, light vignette. |
| Motion | Punch-in zooms on the key moment, freeze-frame + slow-mo on the emotional beat, quick flash cut into the payoff. |
| Captions | 2–4 text beats per video, max 5 words each, every one in `*ASTERISKS*`. Last beat is a meaningful closing line: `*ONE STOP. ONE LIFE.*`, `*HIS HEART STILL BEATS*`. |
| SFX | **Camera shutter + white flash** is the attention sound, on the story beats only: the hook hit at 0.93s (plain footage before it, synced to "Headlights (Slowed)" via "Use this sound" from the reference), the **But** line, the first **Reveal** line, and the ending impact. **Keyboard typing** under every caption while it types, EXCEPT the hook: during the first attention hit the only sounds are the camera shutter + `ding` (`assets/sfx/ding.m4a`), together at 0.93s. `bell-ding` when the heart lands. No pop. Clean effects live in `assets/sfx/`. |
| Ending (every video) | Signature close, from the reference: (1) IMPACT: shutter + the frame blows out (bright pink/orange flash, punch-in to ~1.45× with heavy blur), a ghost echo copy trails offset, decaying camera shake, settle in ~0.6s, (2) the mending-heart emoji sticker (`assets/stickers/mending-heart.png`) slams in ON the impact frame (3.2× + blur → 1× in 0.22s, shakes with the frame, bell rings with it), then pulses, (3) zoom-blur out + dip to black, (4) fade into the video's first frame so the Short loops seamlessly. Built in `videos/gallop-last-wish/index.html` (`#leak`, `#heart`, `#loop`). |
| Music | Sad piano / strings swell, quiet first 3s, peak on the payoff. Royalty-free or generated (vidIQ `generate_music`). |

## Text detail (match the dog clip `videos/gallop-last-wish/`)

Every scene's text is animated, not just typed:

- **Only 3 glowing colors: red, green, yellow (NO blue).** Mix them inside one line like the reference `*STUCK DOG*` (green word + red word): green `.g` = kindness/hero, red `.r` = danger/loss, yellow `.y` = attention/the subject. Rest of the words white.
- **Two-tone lines:** line 1 white, line 2 the key words in neon green or yellow (`*HIS LAST WISH*` / **CHEESE**).
- **Key word gets motion:** pulse (scale 1→1.08 yoyo) or shake (`1 DAY LEFT`) right after it finishes typing.
- **Callout per scene:** circle + curved arrow + 1–2 word label (`GOOD BOY`, `HIS LAST DAY`) drawing in on the subject, then clearing before it moves.
- **Year / place stamp** where it adds truth (`2023`).
- Verdict line two-tone green with glow, typed, on the impact.

## Footage

**Real footage only, of the actual event.** No AI-generated clips, no animation, no stock
"re-enactments". Every video uses the real recording of that real situation.

Workflow per video:

1. Claude picks the story and gives you the YouTube link(s) plus the exact minutes to clip.
2. You download those parts and send them (or drop them in `videos/<name>/assets/footage/`).
3. Claude cuts them to 25–30s in the editing style above.

Credit the original creator in the description, and ask for permission when you can:
reposting someone else's footage can get copyright claims or strikes.

## Text color rule

- Any scene with 2+ caption lines: at least 2 words in different neon colors (red/yellow/green only), rest white.
- The last part (reveal/ending): 3 different colors (e.g. `*RESPECT*` green, `*TRUE` yellow, `HERO*` red).
- Hook style (ref STUCK DOG): 2 words in 2 colors (green + red) ABOVE the subject; glowing green circle on subject; user's curved arrow shape (assets/stickers/arrow-curved-mask.png, used as a CSS mask) solid red fill with thick white outline + red glow, tail at the circle, head pointing at the red word.
- Watermark (faint grey text above the bottom): **LIFE IN FRAME**.

## Retention rules (from the dog-clip review, apply from the next clip)

- **Frame 0 must be sharp and show the danger/subject clearly** — avoid heavy zoom-ins that blur the first frame; use a wider crop where the subject and the situation are both readable.
- **Hook text is on screen from the very first frame** (instant, no type-in), not only from the 0.93s hit. Keep it 2 words.
- **Show the help early.** By ~3s the viewer must see who/what is about to help (not only the victim).
- **1–3s must raise a question** the viewer wants answered (e.g. `*BUT THEN ONE WORKER STEPPED IN*`), written as on-screen text — the channel has no voice-over.
- Pick the freeze frame where the subject's face is clearest, and put the circle/arrow on it.

## Frame layout (from the grandma-clip review)

- **Shrink + raise the video:** inset 864x1536 at (108,96) over a blurred copy of the same shot filling the sides (ffmpeg framed clips `f-*.mp4`); all captions end above y~1600 so YouTube Shorts bottom UI never covers them; watermark sits at the top.
- **Hook line = specific payoff** (who + what happens), e.g. `*GRANDMA SEES HER BABY AND CAN'T STOP CRYING*`, not a generic setup line.

## CLEAN & EMOTIONAL style (review of grandma clip — apply from the NEXT clip; supersedes the neon/circle signature unless the user says otherwise)

Why: viewers recognise the repeated "edit signature" (neon text + green circle + red arrow) within 0.5s and swipe; "WAIT FOR..." reads as a retention trap. Review estimate of Stay-to-Watch for the old style: ~30–38% (an estimate from the review, not real analytics).

- **Remove:** green circle, red arrow, and any "WAIT FOR / WAIT UNTIL THE END" text.
- **Let the footage play naturally**, focused on the face and real emotion.
- **Text = one small, simple subtitle at the bottom** (above the Shorts bottom UI), e.g. `Grandma meeting her grandchild for the first time...` — not full-screen neon multi-line captions that cover the emotion.
- **Get to the point fast:** context must be clear within the first 1.5s. For the grandma story: open at ~2s on grandma leaning in to see the baby, then go straight to the baby on the bed.
- Keep: real footage only, no voice-over, cut every ~3s, blurred-side inset framing, 14–30s.

## Hook method (open with an EVENT, not a preamble) — test 3 variants per story

1. **Open with the event, not the setup.** ❌ "This is a very heartbreaking story…" ✅ "Everyone thought this dog was going to die." Must match the real footage (no invented facts).
2. **Frame 1 = clear problem or emotion** — no logo, long still, or wide shot where you can't tell what's happening.
3. **Don't reveal everything up front** — open with conflict/question, give context and payoff later, without stretching the story.
- For each story, make 3 hook variants (event / question / stakes), same footage, and compare before building the full video. First test: `videos/dog-hook-tests/` (hook-a event, hook-b question, hook-c stakes).

## What works best (user observation, 2026-10-27)

- **Heartbreak / loss content performs best** — someone or an animal dying, a final goodbye, a loyal animal grieving or waiting. Prefer these topics over rescue-with-happy-ending when choosing the next project.
- Keep it respectful: show the goodbye, the waiting, the grief — never graphic death footage. Verify the story (many viral loss clips are staged or reposted): prefer clips with a news source, a named owner/dog, or the original uploader; credit them.
