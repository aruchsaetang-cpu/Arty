# Kuro Oni: Demonic Eye origin short (design, DRAFT for approval)

## Intent
A ~40 s vertical cel horror-action short: the origin of Gojo, "Kuro Oni" (Black Oni), born with the Demonic Eye
(black sclera, glowing white iris: the Aura Monster hero's eyes). Every Yamazaki clan leader must have it; it
stands for Ultra Instinct. Story: newborn -> kid -> teen -> the leadership fight -> adult Kuro Oni.

## Given by the user

- Eyes "like the previous vid" (black sclera, white iris, Aura Monster hero material).
- Wants the fight scene designed first (storyboard). Scare "like a real one".

## Story point (user)
In ep 1 Gojo is betrayed by his own mother.

## Rule (user): Gojo has NO magic
In the fantasy world he never casts a spell. Everything he does is martial art, black bone and the Demonic Eye. Magic belongs to others (the mage, later enemies). Seeing a fireball early is the eyes, not magic. In the series arc, an episode where he sees or uses mana must be dropped or reframed.

## Abilities (given by the user)
- **Demonic Eye / Ultra Instinct:** black sclera, white iris; very fast reflexes, he reacts before the strike.
- **Black bone:** Yamazaki-only. His body is as durable as steel.
- **Aikido (added by user, the aura-farming skill):** calm, almost lazy redirection of an attacker's own force (wrist locks, throws). He never rushes, he never breaks a sweat, which is what makes it look cool.
- **Yamazaki Kyokushin karate:** the clan's style (stances, shin and low kicks, straight punch, kata, full contact).

## Impact rule (user: every punch must look very hard)
Every punch, kick or block that lands gets: a freeze-frame of 2-3 frames, a short camera shake, a white flash on the contact point, a crack or dust burst on the ground or wall, a heavy low sound (sub drop) and a long recoil on the target (the body flies, the rain splashes off). The attacker's fist or shin shows a small shock ring. Smaller hits (slips, blocks) get no shake, only the sound.

## Anime language (user refs: two screen-recorded anime fight clips, kept local only in local_refs/, not committed)
The two clips (vertical, 10.7 s and 11.5 s) are the sound and impact reference. What the frames show, and what we copy as technique (not as artwork):
- **Cuts every 0.3-0.5 s** during a hit sequence (8-15 frames), never a long static shot.
- **Extreme close-ups** (an eye, a clenched jaw, a fist, a piece of skin) alternating with a wide pose.
- **Dutch tilt**: the camera rolls 15-30 degrees on most hit shots and snaps back.
- **Speed lines**: curved radial lines around the figure, and diagonal streaks across the frame.
- **White flash frame** (1-2 frames) at the biggest hit, and a **dark vignette frame** after a block.
- **Hit-stop**: the frame holds 2-3 frames at contact, then the motion explodes.
- **Smear frames**: a blurred drag between two poses, and **floor and debris close-ups** (cracks, plank splinters, rain sparks).
- Hand-drawn on-twos feel (12 drawings per second) with a heavy ink or halftone texture on the skin.
Sound (I cannot hear the clips; this is from the waveform): dense bright hits, the spectral centre falls from about 4 kHz on the first hits to about 1 kHz on the heavy tail, peaks about 0.6-0.75. Plan: use the clips' own audio as the impact bed, layered with the sub drop from the impact rule. The source audio licence is unknown: fine for a private reference cut, check before publishing.

## Gojo's martial art: Yamazaki Kyokushin + Aikido (hard and soft)
Research note: I could not confirm that any Lookism character is canonically a Kyokushin or aikido user (Vin Jin's style is Kudo/judo and ssireum; Daniel Park copies moves). So this is a design built from the real arts, not a Lookism fact. Marked as my design.

**Principle:** Kyokushin is the hard half (full contact, break the opponent). Aikido is the soft half (never meet force with force, turn the attacker's own force against him). Gojo shows both in every sequence: a calm, small redirect (soft), then one huge hit (hard). That contrast is the aura.

**Kyokushin moves (hard), the poses to animate:**
- Stances: sanchin-dachi (hourglass stance, feet pigeon-toed, knees in) as his idle; kiba-dachi (horse stance) when he takes a hit; zenkutsu-dachi (long forward stance) for the finisher.
- Gyaku-zuki: reverse straight punch from the rear hand, hip rotation, the big finisher.
- Mawashi-geri gedan: low roundhouse kick to the thigh, and the shin-on-shin block that rings like steel (black bone).
- Hiza-geri: knee strike in the clinch.
- Mae-geri: front kick to the chest.
- Ibuki: the forced exhale through the nose, with the stomach tight, before he takes a hit.
- Kata: sanchin (slow, tense) in the teen dojo shot.

**Aikido moves (soft):**
- Irimi (enter) and tenkan (turn): step into or around the line of the attack; he is never where the hit lands.
- Kote-gaeshi: wrist throw; the attacker flips himself.
- Irimi-nage: the entering throw, arm across the neck and chest.
- Shiho-nage: four-direction throw with the arm over the head.
- Ikkyo and nikyo: arm pins and wrist locks for the 'hands in pockets' beat.
- Redirect: one attacker's punch pulls him into the next attacker.

**Where each appears (war fight, shots 5-9):**
- 6: tenkan plus kote-gaeshi (soft), straighten sleeve.
- 6: mawashi-geri gedan (hard) into a charging fighter; the shin rings.
- 8: silhouette walk, irimi through the crowd; no strikes, only redirects.
- 9: one gyaku-zuki from zenkutsu-dachi, with the full impact rule.

**Build note:** these are the hardest animations in the film (feet planted, hip rotation, hand paths). Use the strongest model for the choreography, and plant every foot with IK as in the Aura Monster rig.

## Gojo's past (user): a childhood like hell
His mother only treats him well when he hurts people. He is loved only when he fights and wins. So he learns that love = violence, and he almost kills everything he sees (the reason he is called Kuro Oni, the Black Oni). Everything he wins, he wins for her smile.
- She is cold and blank to him by default; the warm smile is the reward when he hurts someone.
- He never stops on his own; only she can stop him (shot 10) because her approval is the only thing he wants.
- The poison scene (shot 11): his Demonic Eye sees the poison in the tea. He drinks anyway, for the smile. The tragedy is that he chooses it.
- Series use: in the fantasy world he does not understand kindness, and the people who are kind to him without him fighting are the thing he cannot read.
Content note: fiction about abuse and conditioned violence; keep it implied (looks, silence, one image of a bloodied boy), not graphic.

## Unemotional fight look (user ref image): ink silhouettes
When Gojo fights he shows no emotion: face blank, mouth shut, eyes flat, breathing even, no shout, no grin. To sell it, the biggest hits cut to a pure ink-silhouette frame: flat solid black shapes on a flat white background (no shading, no face detail, only hair strands and the fingers of a flung hand), with an ink splatter spraying from the point of impact. The enemy is the one thrown or breaking in the silhouette; Gojo is a still black shape. Hold 3-6 frames, then cut back to colour.
Use at: the big hits of shot 5-9 (each count number in shot 9), the shin check in shot 3, and the counter-punch in shot 17. Sound: the impact sub drop, then 2 frames of silence.
Compose note: a silhouette frame is made in post (threshold the render, white background, add an ink-splatter overlay), so it needs no extra 3D work.
Prototype note: not in the current 3D prototype; added in the full version.
