# KURO ONI
## Episode 1: "The Smile"

---

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
Black bone: a body as hard as steel. It can stop even a katana, if he is awake.
The Demonic Eye: it sees a blow before it lands.
Karate: hard. It breaks.
Aikido: soft. It turns your force against you.
He has no name. Everyone calls him Young Master.

---

**INT. YAMAZAKI HALL, JAPAN - DAY**

A long tatami hall. Thirty clan members kneel in rows. A thin BOY stands alone in front of them. His eyes are black, with white irises. His MOTHER, the father's mistress, stands beside him, cold and still. His FATHER sits slumped to one side, bandaged and broken after losing to the strongest gang in Korea. He does not look at the boy.

ELDER
(whispering)
The Demonic Eye... What is the boy's name?

The father says nothing. He never named him.

From that day, the whole clan calls him Young Master.

A bigger boy lies bloody at the boy's feet. The clan flinches. The mother kneels and smiles at her son for the first time. She takes his hand.

MOTHER
Good boy.

The boy looks only at her smile.

---

**EXT. YAMAZAKI GATE - DAY**

Normal children play outside the gate. The boy walks toward them. His mother steps in front of him and slaps him.

BOY
Can I play with them?

MOTHER
A future clan leader will not play with those kids.

He lowers his head and walks back inside.

---

**INT. DOJO - DAY**

The boy is now a teen. The MASTER, very old, with a white topknot and a long white beard, attacks. The teen turns, takes his wrist, and throws him. The master lands and nods.

MASTER
Young Master. Soft first. Then hard.

The teen does a karate kata and breaks a steel bat on his shin. On the veranda, his mother watches without any expression. When the bat breaks, she smiles.

---

**EXT. MUD - RAIN**

His father's black cloak lies in the mud.

MASTER (V.O.)
The war took your father, Young Master. He never said your name.

He lifts the cloak and puts it on.

---

**EXT. BURNING STREET, KOREA - NIGHT**

Fires burn on both sides. A hundred fighters in black suits fill the street. The man in the cloak stands alone, his face blank.

ENEMY
Kill him!

They rush him, one at a time. He hits each once and straightens his sleeve. His eyes turn full white. He slips every blow. He walks through the fire, a black shape. Nobody can touch him.

ENEMY
(crawling back)
Kuro Oni... the Black Oni.

The last twenty rush him. He throws one straight punch. The rain splits.

Every clan leader kneels. His fist is raised over the last one. He does not stop.

LEADER
Please...

His mother steps into the edge of the frame.

MOTHER
Young Master.

He freezes.

---

**INT. YAMAZAKI HOUSE, JAPAN - NIGHT**

He is home, asleep on the floor, worn out. Moonlight at the window. His mother slides the door open. She holds a katana. She wants the Yamazaki and its power, and the heir with the Demonic Eye stands in her way. Black bone does not work while he sleeps.

She stabs him in the back.

His white eyes open. He does not turn.

MOTHER
(smiling, warm)
Good boy.

He could kill her in one move. He looks at her smile, and he does not. He sinks to one knee.

GOJO (V.O.)
I fought for a name my father never gave me.

He looks up at her. She turns away.

GOJO
(a whisper)
...Damn it.

His eyes close. Black.

---

**EXT. MEADOW, A NEW WORLD - DAWN**

A human face. Eyes open: black, with white irises. He wakes in the grass. Floating lights. A castle on a hill. Two moons. He looks at his hands.

GOJO (V.O.)
No clan. No mother. No name.

---

**EXT. ROAD - DAY**

A young magic KNIGHT blocks the road, a grimoire at his belt.

KNIGHT
A man with no grimoire? Burn him!

He throws a ball of fire. Gojo's eyes follow it before the cast. He slips it, steps in, and throws one straight punch. The knight flies back.

The knight looks up from the ground at one white eye.

KNIGHT
Black-eyed demon... who are you?

GOJO
(quietly)
...Gojo.

**TITLE: KURO ONI**
