# KURO ONI, Episode 1: 30-second script (hook, context, reveal)

Naming rule: the story never gives him a name. The clan says "Young Master" to his face. Everyone else, in fear, calls him "Kuro Oni".
Structure: HOOK (0-3 s) shows the biggest image first. CONTEXT (3-19 s) tells who he is and what he wants. REVEAL (19-30 s) is the betrayal and the new world.
About 38 words of dialogue and narration.

---

## HOOK (0-3 s)

**EXT. BURNING STREET, KOREA - NIGHT**
Fires. A hundred men in black suits around one man in a black cloak, his face blank. One straight punch. The rain splits.

NARRATOR (V.O.)
He defeated a hundred men, alone. They call him Kuro Oni, the Black Oni, out of fear.

## CONTEXT (3-19 s)

**INT. YAMAZAKI HALL, JAPAN - DAY (3-6 s)**
Thirty clan members kneel. A thin boy with black eyes and white irises stands before them. His father sits slumped to one side and will not look at him.

NARRATOR (V.O.)
He has no name. Everyone calls him Young Master.

**EXT. YAMAZAKI GATE - DAY (6-8 s)**
Children play outside. He steps toward them. His mother slaps him.

MOTHER
Young Master, a future clan leader will not play with those kids.

**INT. DOJO (8-11 s)**
The old master throws him. He learns it: aikido, then Kyokushin karate. He breaks a steel bat on his shin. On the veranda his mother, blank until now, smiles.

MASTER
I can't believe you've mastered it already, Young Master.

**EXT. MUD, RAIN (11-14 s)**
His father's black cloak lies in the mud. He puts it on.

NARRATOR (V.O.)
He fought for one thing: his father's voice saying his name.

**EXT. BURNING STREET (14-19 s)**
His eyes go full white. He slips every blow, one against a hundred. Every clan leader kneels.

ENEMY
(whispering, in fear)
Kuro Oni...

## REVEAL (19-30 s)

**INT. YAMAZAKI HOUSE, JAPAN - NIGHT (19-22 s)**
Home, asleep. His mother slides the door open with a katana. Black bone does not work while he sleeps. She stabs him in the back. His white eyes open.

MOTHER
(smiling)
Young Master.

**INT. YAMAZAKI HOUSE (22-24 s)**
He looks at her smile and does not fight back. He sinks to one knee.

YOUNG MASTER
...Damn it.

Black.

**FIRST-PERSON VIEW - UNKNOWN PLACE (24-30 s)**
We open our eyes in grass under two moons. We sit up. A town. People use fire magic: a street lamp lit with a spark, children throwing small fireballs. The view follows one fireball before the throw. We look at our hands. Empty.

## Notes
- Hook shows the climax first, then the film explains how he got there; the reveal (the mother, then the new world) lands in the last 10 seconds.
- Cut from the 60 s version: the newborn-eyes explainer, the bigger boy, the clan-leader kneel scene as its own beat, the narrator's ability list, "No clan. No mother. No name."
- If the abilities must still be explained, say them in the hook: "Born with black bone and the Demonic Eye." (adds 3 s; cut the slap to make room).
